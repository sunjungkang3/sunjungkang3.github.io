Graduate students

We are looking for motivated students interested in intelligent and networked systems, including multi-agent AI, communication networks, distributed learning, optimization, and information freshness.

Prospective students are encouraged to send their CV by email.

ISN Lab에서는 지능형 네트워크 시스템, 멀티에이전트 AI, 통신 네트워크, 분산 학습, 최적화, 정보 신선도 분야에 관심 있는 대학원생을 모집합니다.

관심 있는 학생은 이메일로 CV를 보내주시기 바랍니다.

Research fit

Strong applicants are curious about both theory and systems, and are comfortable learning mathematical modeling, optimization, machine learning, or communication/networking tools.
