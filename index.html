About us

Intelligence that works across networks.

The Intelligent Systems and Networks Laboratory (ISN Lab) studies information exchange, decision-making, collaboration, and resource optimization in intelligent and networked systems. We develop theoretical, optimization-based, and learning-based approaches for efficient, reliable, and adaptive networked intelligence.

Multi-Agent and AI Systems

Learning, coordination, routing, and decision-making among interacting intelligent agents.

Information Freshness and Networked Systems

Timely information delivery, Age of Information, and control of dynamic networked systems.

Communication and Resource Optimization

Optimization and learning for wireless, edge, satellite, and distributed computing systems.
